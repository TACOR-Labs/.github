## Overview

TACOR-Labs is an independent engineering research initiative focused on understanding, designing, and improving technical systems through computation, mathematical modeling, and experimentation.

It brings together ideas from engineering, computer science, applied mathematics, and the natural sciences to explore how complex systems can be analyzed, developed, and refined.

The initiative emphasizes a fundamental understanding of underlying principles, systematic experimentation, and the development of practical technical knowledge.

Rather than focusing on a single application, TACOR-Labs provides an umbrella for research projects that investigate different aspects of computational engineering and intelligent technical systems.

## Projects

TACOR-Labs serves as an umbrella for individual research and development projects.

Each project has its own objectives, technical documentation, implementation, and development process.

### MORPH / EVOLVE

The first research project explores computational design and algorithmic optimization.

It begins with mathematical foundations and experimental evaluation, with the longer-term goal of investigating optimization methods in engineering models and simulated systems.

For its research questions, technical details, experiments, and current development status, see the project's own repository documentation.

## Research Principles

| Principle                  | Meaning                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First Principles**       | Understand the fundamental concepts behind a method before treating it as a tool.             |
| **Scientific Curiosity**   | Investigate questions systematically rather than relying on assumptions.                      |
| **Measurable Progress**    | Define criteria that make results observable and comparable.                                  |
| **Transparent Evaluation** | Document methods, assumptions, limitations, and findings.                                     |
| **Reproducibility**        | Structure experiments so that their results can be checked and repeated.                      |
| **Continuous Learning**    | Treat implementation, testing, and failure analysis as opportunities to deepen understanding. |

## Approach

TACOR-Labs follows an iterative approach to engineering research:

```text
Question
   ↓
Understanding
   ↓
Modeling
   ↓
Implementation
   ↓
Experimentation
   ↓
Evaluation
   ↓
Refinement
```

The process is adapted to the needs of each project. Mathematical analysis, simulation, practical implementation, and experimental evaluation may all contribute to understanding a problem.

Complexity is introduced progressively, with an emphasis on validating assumptions and learning from results.

## Long-Term Direction

TACOR-Labs is intended to grow through individual projects, accumulated knowledge, and progressively more challenging technical investigations.

Potential directions include computational engineering, optimization, physical simulation, robotics, automation, and intelligent systems.

These areas represent an evolving field of interest rather than a fixed set of commitments. Future projects and priorities will be shaped by learning, experimentation, and demonstrated technical feasibility.

## Engineering Philosophy

Engineering is more than making something work. It also means understanding why it works, identifying where it fails, and determining how its behavior can be evaluated and improved.

TACOR-Labs approaches technical development as a continuous process of learning, modeling, experimentation, and refinement.
